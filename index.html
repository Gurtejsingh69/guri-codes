The Impact of Single-Use Plastics on Waste Management in India

Cheap, light and used once, then thrown away. See how everyday plastic clogs our drains, rivers and landfills, and what we can do about it.

What are single-use plastics?

Products that are generally used once and then discarded, such as plastic bags, disposable cups, plates, straws and food packaging.

🚰

Blocked drains

Plastic waste can choke drains and worsen waterlogging.

🌊

Polluted rivers & land

Discarded plastic ends up in water bodies and on open land.

🐄

Harm to animals

Animals can be hurt by plastic they swallow or get tangled in.

🗑

Overflowing landfills

Plastic takes a very long time to break down, so it stays for years.

Key facts

Policy milestones and findings from the project's literature review.

2016

Plastic Waste Management Rules

Introduced by the Government of India to improve collection, segregation and management of plastic waste.

1 July 2022

Restrictions begin

Restrictions on identified single-use plastic items came into effect.

UNEP

Reduce & reuse

The UN Environment Programme stresses cutting unnecessary plastic use and promoting reusable alternatives.

What you can do

Small everyday actions add up to better waste management.

  • Avoid unnecessary plastic bags and carry a reusable bag.
  • Use reusable bottles and containers instead of disposable ones.
  • Segregate waste so recyclables are kept separate.
  • Dispose of waste correctly and keep your surroundings clean.
